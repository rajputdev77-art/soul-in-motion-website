## Why these specific changes

- **The "8 products live" line** turns visitors into curious clickers in 2 seconds.
- **🇮🇳 flag** signals you're an Indian builder (an underserved niche on Gumroad — Western buyers actively look for non-US makers).
- **soulinmotion.studio in the link slot** preserves brand equity (you can change Gumroad slugs later; you can't easily change the .studio domain).
- **Single bio link, not Linktree** — Linktree pages have ~30% drop-off vs. a direct site link to your own storefront.

---

## What NOT to put in the bio

- Buzzwords like "growth hacker", "founder", "entrepreneur" — they signal sameness
- Multiple emojis per line — feels noisy at the small mobile font size
- A long quote — bio space is too tiny
