— FAQ

The questions we actually get.

Still chewing on something? Ping us in the waitlist note — a real person responds.

How is Relay different from generic OCR or Zapier-style scrapers?
OCR gives you text. Relay gives you structured fields — with confidence scores, validation rules, and a write-back to your CRM or TMS. We're trained on the documents your industry actually runs on (rate confs, MLS sheets, BOLs), not generic invoices. The output is a record, not a string.
What happens when the AI gets a field wrong?
Every field comes back with a confidence score. You set the threshold — say, 95% — and anything below routes to a one-click human review queue. We've measured a 99.2% straight-through rate on common templates; the remaining 0.8% gets eyeballs in under a minute.
How long does onboarding actually take?
For standard templates and a supported CRM (HubSpot, Follow Up Boss, McLeod, Salesforce, Pipedrive): ~90 minutes with our team on a Zoom. For custom forms or non-standard destinations: 3–7 days, including training on a sample set of your documents.
Where does our data live? Is it used to train models?
Data is processed in us-east-1 on isolated tenancy and deleted from extraction infrastructure within 24 hours. Your documents are never used to train shared models. SOC 2 Type II is in progress; we'll share the report under NDA.
What if we go over the monthly document limit?
On Operator we soft-cap at 500 and notify you at 80%. On Throughput, overage runs at $0.09 per doc — no surprise bills, no service suspension. You can also upshift mid-month, prorated.
Can it handle handwriting, signatures, and bad scans?
Yes — that's most of what our customers send us. Faxed BOLs, signed riders, handwritten amendments. We score those lower in confidence (correctly), but extraction works. We've intentionally not built around the assumption of clean PDFs.
Do you offer a pilot before we commit?
Every account gets a 30-day pilot, no card up front. You bring 100 of your real documents and a destination; we set it up; you measure. If it doesn't pay back inside the pilot, you walk.
