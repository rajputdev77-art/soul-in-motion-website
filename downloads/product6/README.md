## How to Customize (10 Minutes per Brand)

1. Open `Relay-Landing.html` in any text editor.
2. Find-and-replace these terms:
   - `Relay` → your brand name
   - `BOLS, CONTRACTS, MLS SHEETS` → your verticals
   - `Stop retyping documents into your CRM` → your headline
   - `inbound-docs.yaml` → your workflow filename
3. Replace the SVG hero illustration with screenshots of your actual app (or keep the abstract workflow — it works even without your product being shipped yet).
4. Change the brand color: search `#E8A04A` and replace with your accent color (3 occurrences).
5. Wire the "Join waitlist" button to your form (Tally, Typeform, Buttondown, anything).
6. Save. Deploy to Vercel / Netlify / GitHub Pages. Done.

## How to Deploy in 60 Seconds

**Easiest path — Vercel:**
1. Sign up at [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) with GitHub.
2. Drag the `Relay-Landing.html` file onto the dashboard. (Or push to a repo and connect.)
3. Vercel gives you a URL like `relay.vercel.app`. Done.

**Even simpler — Cloudflare Pages:**
1. Create a folder. Drop the HTML file in. Rename to `index.html`.
2. Go to [pages.cloudflare.com](https://pages.cloudflare.com) → Create project → Upload assets.
3. Drag your folder. Get a URL. Done.
